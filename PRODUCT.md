## Platform

web

## Users

Families and people of all ages in and around Castelfranco Veneto (TV), Italy — both people with disabilities ("diverse abilità") and able-bodied people ("normodotate") — who want to take up swimming or dance together, in the same classes rather than separate adaptive tracks.

## Product Purpose

A.S.D. WE SPORT is a newly founded sports association (ASD) bringing swimming and dance classes to Castelfranco Veneto with inclusion built into the class format itself: people with and without disabilities train side by side. Success at this stage means communicating the project clearly enough to recruit founding members/families before classes start.

## Positioning

Inclusion is the whole point, not an add-on: classes are mixed by design, with people of all abilities training together rather than in separate adaptive programs. This is the association's stated differentiator from other local swim/dance clubs.

## Operating Context

- Status: pre-launch. The legal entity (ASD) exists; classes have not started yet.
- Location: Castelfranco Veneto (TV), Italy.
- Initial disciplines: swimming ("We Nuoto") and dance ("We Ballo"), with the brand explicitly designed to expand into further disciplines over time.
- The site's current copy still carries placeholders that must be filled with real facts before launch, not invented: course details/schedules/age ranges (Disciplines section), street address, phone, email (Contact section and footer), and C.F./P.IVA (footer).
- Content and UI copy are in Italian; preserve this unless the user says otherwise.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static marketing/landing site built with Astro (no CMS, no backend currently wired to the contact form).
- Contact form currently posts to `#` with no backend — submission handling is an open technical gap, not yet decided.
- Undecided/open: final course schedules, age brackets, pricing, exact venue address, phone, email, and C.F./P.IVA.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: A.S.D. WE SPORT. The "we" in the name is explicitly tied to the inclusion/union concept (see Values: "legame", "inclusione", "fluidità").
- Confirmed sub-brand system: each additional discipline the association adds in the future gets its own logo declination (variation) while keeping the same underlying values — this is a binding brand rule, not a placeholder idea, per explicit user confirmation.
- Existing wordmark/logo asset at `public/assets/img/logo.png` (referenced in the footer).

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, case studies, press, pricing, or enrollment numbers exist yet — the association is pre-launch. Do not fabricate any of these.
- Real image assets already in use: pool, dance, hands/inclusion, swimmer, underwater (see `public/assets/img/`).
- Contact details (address, phone, email, C.F./P.IVA) are not yet known/confirmed — currently bracketed placeholders in the code; future work must carry them as explicit unknowns, not invent values.

## Product Principles

1. Inclusion is structural, not decorative: mixed-ability classes are the product, not a marketing angle layered on top.
2. Honesty over polish for unconfirmed facts: placeholder data (address, phone, pricing, schedules) stays visibly unresolved rather than being filled with invented specifics.
3. The brand is designed to grow: new disciplines are expected, each getting its own logo declination under one shared set of values.
4. Pre-launch tone: communicate a credible, concrete new project to prospective founding families, not an already-established track record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Given the product's core audience explicitly includes people with disabilities, accessibility is a product requirement, not a generic best practice: content, forms, and imagery should not assume able-bodied users by default.
